# Platform playbooks

Read the requested platform's section. These checklists identify surfaces to inspect; they do not guarantee field availability or entitlement. The source links are research entry points drawn from the originating workflow. Reopen the relevant official documentation and inspect the actual account before asserting a current limit, image requirement, or feature rule. Record the date and unresolved conflicts. Login walls are evidence gaps.

## LinkedIn

- Core: name, headline, About, contact website, portrait, cover.
- Supporting profile review: Featured items and order, Experience descriptions, top skills, recommendations, existing follower controls. Editing these is additional scope, not implied by a headline rewrite.
- Let the headline lead with an understandable association and selected proof. Use About for project explanations and process. Preserve accurate titles, employers, employment dates, and location.
- Check real paragraph blocks after inserting About or Experience text. Verify website type and actual displayed label; a proposed custom label may not exist.
- Prefer relevant existing Featured material. Verify preview and item ordering after save. Never send recommendation requests as part of an audit.
- Review existing cover before proposing replacement. For new artwork, check current dimensions, avatar overlap, and actual narrow/desktop crops.
- Inspect whether saving Experience triggers a network announcement and resolve that side effect within approval. Follow-primary and follower visibility are separate account decisions.

Official starting points: [cover image](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a568217/adding-or-changing-the-background-photo-on-your-profile?lang=en), [website links](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a548010/add-a-website-to-your-profile?lang=en), [custom button](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1490317?lang=en-US).

## YouTube

- Core: channel name, unchanged handle unless requested, description, link titles and order, portrait, banner.
- Supporting review: Home sections, trailer, returning-viewer spotlight, public playlists. Inventory visible options without modifying private/unlisted playlists or publishing content.
- Use the description to explain what the creator actually makes or shows. Put the intended next step in the link order where appropriate, accurately distinguish free and paid destinations, and verify the resulting order.
- Preserve business email, watermark, and existing images unless included in the request. Channel description edits do not authorize upload-default or old video-description edits.
- Verify Studio save/publish state and the channel display separately. For new banner art, check desktop/mobile/TV previews and safe areas against current official guidance.

Official starting points: [channel profile](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2657964?hl=en), [branding](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525?hl=en), [handles](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11585688?hl=en).

## Instagram

- Core: name, username, bio, profile links, avatar. Inspect category and contact display only where exposed. Review existing pinned posts and Highlights if relevant.
- Keep the same association in compact form with readable proof. Check the full expanded bio and the initially collapsed view.
- Do not design a banner unless the actual account exposes that surface. Existing Highlights are content, not a fictional banner substitute.
- Website editing may depend on the app surface. If web explicitly defers links to mobile, hand off that exact step. Keep the unresolved destination visible in the report.
- Accounts Center may synchronize identity across services. Do not allow an unintended cross-account update. Category, public contact, professional conversion, and cross-account synchronization require their own scope.

Official starting point: [Instagram Help Center](https://help.instagram.com/). Navigate current profile-editing and account-sync guidance there; verify account-specific controls in the app.

## TikTok

- Core: nickname, username, bio, avatar, website and connected links where available. Review pins only when useful to the brief.
- Preserve the association and exact proof metric while compressing. Use the live editor count; local emoji counts can differ.
- Check nickname and username restrictions separately. Do not infer one cooldown from the other or repeatedly rename to fix stale rendering.
- Website eligibility and web/mobile controls must be verified for this account. If official sources disagree about eligibility, retain the discrepancy. Do not convert to Business solely to expose a link.
- Do not create a banner unless a real supported field exists. Account-health checks, appeals, deletion, security reviews, and posting tests are separate work.

Official starting points: [profile setup](https://support.tiktok.com/en/getting-started/setting-up-your-profile), [profile links](https://support.tiktok.com/en/getting-started/setting-up-your-profile/linking-another-social-media-account), [account types](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/growing-your-audience/switching-to-a-creator-or-business-account), [username](https://support.tiktok.com/en/getting-started/setting-up-your-profile/changing-your-username).

## X

- Core: name, actual handle, bio, website, portrait, header. Retain accurate location unless changing it is requested.
- Verify account ownership even when its handle differs from the other platforms. Cross-platform consistency does not require a username migration.
- Check current bio limit and saved text. Check link destination and line wrapping separately from character count.
- Review existing portrait/header at small and narrow sizes, including avatar overlap. Retain effective assets.
- A missing pin is a recommendation opportunity, not permission to post. Professional category, paid features, and account conversion are additional decisions.

Official starting points: [profile customization](https://help.x.com/en/managing-your-account/how-to-customize-your-profile), [image upload guidance](https://help.x.com/en/managing-your-account/common-issues-when-uploading-profile-photo).
