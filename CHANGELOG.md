## v0.1.0-beta.1 · 2026-09-09

First private preview of All-profile.

### Included

- Profile review and writing for LinkedIn, YouTube channels, Instagram, TikTok, and X.
- A shared positioning method: recognizable association, relevant proof, and a natural next step.
- Guidance for useful emoji, trustworthy claims, and platform-appropriate copy.
- Approved setup, saved-field verification, and clear handoffs for blocked or mobile-only steps.
- Codex display metadata, a read-only text counter, and six automated tests.
- Installation guidance, example prompts, and documented evaluation results.

### Refined during review

- Clearer boundaries between profiles and unrelated content or account-health tasks.
- Public profile text kept separate from verification bookkeeping.
- Safer resumption when older drafts differ from newer saved records.

### Known limits

Other agent hosts have not been installation-tested. A distribution license remains undecided.
